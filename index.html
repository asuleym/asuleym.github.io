Arzu Su

Graduate Research Assistant, Kent State University

Hi, I'm Arzu. I study how people talk to AI systems, and what they leave out. At Kent State University I lead an IRB-approved interview study on self-censorship and AI adoption among English, Azerbaijani and Russian speakers: when and why people change or hold back what they type into a large language model.

I care most about what this means for medicine. A clinical decision-support tool can only work with the information it is given, so I want to understand how clinicians and patients decide what to share with AI, and how to build tools they can be honest with. I am applying to PhD programs to keep working on these questions.

Research interests

Research

Kent State

Self-censorship and AI adoption across languages

Graduate Research Assistant · Feb 2026 to present · IRB-approved, in progress

An interview study with English, Azerbaijani and Russian speakers about how they edit, soften or withhold what they tell large language models, and how that shapes trust in AI. I code the multilingual transcripts in NVivo, run thematic analysis, and analyze the quantitative data in R and SAS. I also run the study's consent process, data privacy and participant communication.
