Arzu Suleymanova

Research Assistant, Kent State University

Hi, I'm Arzu. I study how people use AI systems, and what they choose not to tell them. I'm a research assistant at Kent State University, where I work with Dr. Lala Hajibayova on a qualitative study of privacy perception and self-censorship in human-AI interaction.

With Dr. Hajibayova I also study how immigrants and non-native speakers use generative AI, work that was accepted at ASIS&T 2026. Beyond conversational AI, I have built machine learning models of aging biomarkers, and I'm interested in biosignals and in data visualization that helps people understand AI. I am applying to PhD programs to keep working on human-centered AI.

Research interests

  • Conversational AI and LLM systems
  • Human-AI and human-robot interaction
  • Trust, privacy and responsible AI
  • Interactive machine learning systems
  • Biosignal analysis and computational neuroscience
  • Longevity and biomarkers of aging
  • Data visualization for human-centered AI

Before research

I worked in data and product roles. I'm a part-time data analyst at Yieldmo, and before that I was a technical product manager at the EdTech startup Audo and a business analyst at EtaVista. In 2020 I co-founded Savadly, an online marketplace for live one-to-one lessons and video courses, where I led product design and user research.

Languages

I studied language interpretation and translation. Azerbaijani is my first language, and I also speak English, Russian and Turkish. Working across languages is a big part of why I care about how people talk to machines.

Kent State

Privacy perception and self-censorship in human-AI interaction

Research Assistant · Feb 2026 to present · Supervisor: Dr. Lala Hajibayova

A qualitative study of 39 Eurasian-origin postgraduate and postdoctoral researchers based in the US, EU and UK. I lead participant recruitment, built the Qualtrics survey, run the semi-structured interviews, and transcribe, code and analyze the data in Python. I also work on the literature review.
